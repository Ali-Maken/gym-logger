Fix the daytime

No counting, no banned food, no plan you'll rebound from. One change, made at breakfast and lunch.

You eat almost nothing until evening and then most of the day's food arrives at once. That isn't a willpower failure — it's arithmetic. By 8pm you're running on one roti and some fruit, and no one negotiates well with a pizza in that state.

You don't need to want the pizza less. You need to arrive at 8pm not starving.

02 / The fix

Four blocks

Morning
Add eggs to the roti

Two or three, any style. The roti alone leaves you empty by 11. Eggs plus roti holds until lunch, and this single swap does more than anything else on the page.

Office · midday
Actually eat, even when busy

This is the block that breaks everything downstream. Chicken, daal, chana or keema with your roti, and dahi alongside if you can.

If you can't stop to eat properly, keep a backup in your bag so skipping isn't the default: boiled eggs, a tin of tuna, roasted chana, or a yogurt.

Evening · before home
Eat something before the ordering decision

Fruit is good, fruit with a handful of nuts or some dahi is better. A small planned snack around 6 is what stops the 9pm decision from being made on an empty stomach.

Night
Dinner stays yours

Home food most nights — roti with whatever's cooked, and put something protein-heavy on the plate first.

Pizza or burgers a couple of times a week, still the same four or five slices. Not a cheat, not earned, not compensated for. Just twice instead of most nights.

04 / Drinks

The invisible one

Liquid calories don't register as eating and don't make you full, which makes them the easiest thing on this page to change and the easiest to overlook.

  • Sugar in chai — the biggest one, because of how often it happens. Drop it by half, then to zero over a few weeks. Going cold turkey makes chai taste wrong and you'll just quit the attempt.
  • Cola and juice — with pizza especially. Switch to water or a diet version. This alone is a meaningful chunk of your week.
  • Lassi and milkshakes — sweet lassi is dessert. Salted or plain is fine.
  • Water — keep a bottle at your desk. Thirst reads as hunger surprisingly often.
05 / Guardrails

Things not to do

Because they cause the bounce
  • No crash dieting. You already know how that story ends for you, and you're right. Severe restriction is what builds the rebound, not weak character.
  • Nothing is banned. The moment a food becomes forbidden it becomes the only thing you think about. Pizza stays on the menu.
  • Don't skip meals to "save up" for a big dinner. That's the exact pattern this page exists to break.
  • Don't weigh yourself daily. Weight swings a kilo or two on water alone. Once a week, same morning, or not at all — go by how clothes fit and what the gym log says.
  • Don't change everything at once. Start with breakfast eggs and eating at the office. Add the drinks piece in a couple of weeks, once the first part is automatic.

A bad week doesn't undo anything. Nothing here needs to be done perfectly — it needs to be done most days, for months.
